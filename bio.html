The short version

I turn small ideas into clickable things.

I’m Ganesh Kumar. By day I ship software. On weekends I ship experiments: small games, tiny tools, and ideas that were supposed to take an afternoon. I like building in the open, in public, where the commit history can embarrass me properly.

My current obsession is the build loop: a continuous cycle where my AI collaborator Pip does an unreasonable amount of typing and I do an unreasonable amount of supervising. It is the most productive I have ever been, and the least I have ever typed.

How I work

The loop

Every project here runs the same playbook:

  1. Small idea. If it doesn’t fit in a weekend, it gets smaller.
  2. Build fast. Pip writes the code; I make the decisions and the coffee.
  3. Ship it. Real URLs, real users, real bug reports.
  4. Learn in public. The wins go on X. The failures go in the comics.

Current loop output: codevu.com, ten daily games and counting.
